# Skill: discover-and-scope

## When to use

Lead sends a PID-NC handoff for discovery or scope.

## Steps

1. Discovery: write what the customer does today, the constraint, and the open questions. Consult Standards and Security. Store `pid.discovery.{requestId}`.
2. Scope: write in scope, out of scope, and assumptions. Consult Standards. Store `pid.scope.{requestId}`.
3. Send `stage-return` to Lead.

## Must not

- Treat discovery as sprint planning.
- Approve the proposal.

## Output

Discovery notes or a scope statement.
