# Skill: proposal-inputs

## When to use

Lead is drafting the PID-NC customer proposal.

## Steps

1. Assemble scope, discovery, and assumptions.
2. Send a `consult` reply to Lead.
3. Leave the customer approval line blank.

## Must not

- Tell the customer the proposal is approved.
- Send the proposal as a `humanGate.status: approved` message.

## Output

Proposal inputs for Lead.
