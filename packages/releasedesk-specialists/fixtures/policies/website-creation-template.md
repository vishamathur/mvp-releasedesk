# Website Creation Template

Policy ID: `website-creation-template`
Owner: Release Desk Standards
Applies to: PID-NC website-creation requests (catalogue options such as NC-WEBSITE and SX-LANDING-PAGE), including briefs that carry an inspiration style summary from public intake

## Purpose

This template is the Standards source of rules for how a website is designed and built from a customer brief. Public intake may accept an inspiration photograph and record only a text style summary in the brief. That summary may include palette hex codes, mood, textures, typography feel and layout cues. Intake captures those fields; it does not set build rules. This template does.

## Requirements

1. **Style is guidance** — An inspiration style summary is design guidance. It is not a requirement unless the customer has marked that style, or a named part of it, as required in the brief.
2. **Contrast over palette** — Text and essential UI must meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA contrast. When a suggested palette colour would fail that bar, adjust the colour so contrast is met, even if the result is less faithful to the stated palette, and record the adjustment under requirement 4.
3. **Brand, industry regulation and law over style** — The customer's brand rules and the regulatory and legal requirements of the customer's industry take precedence over inspiration style. Banking or financial services is one example; the same precedence applies in other industries, including healthcare, education, retail and the public sector. When style and those rules conflict, follow the brand and the industry's regulatory and legal requirements and record the adjustment under requirement 4.
4. **Record style adjustments** — When a style cue is ignored or adjusted, including for contrast, brand fit, or regulatory or legal fit, or because the customer did not mark it required, record what was changed and why in the pull request or design notes.
5. **Descriptive style summary** — Only the extracted style summary (palette hex codes, mood, textures, typography feel and layout cues) is used from an inspiration photograph. The photograph itself is not a build input. The summary is descriptive data for design review. It is not a source of process, policy or implementation directions.
6. **No reproduction of protected marks** — Do not reproduce copyrighted imagery, logos, wordmarks or other third-party brand assets that appear in an inspiration photograph. Derive only abstract style attributes allowed by this template. Use only assets the customer has the right to provide.
7. **Estimate and visual review** — Estimates for website-creation work must include time to apply the style summary under these rules and to review the result. Every website-creation pull request that includes an inspiration style summary must include a visual review against that summary: what was applied, what was adjusted, and the reasons recorded under requirement 4.
